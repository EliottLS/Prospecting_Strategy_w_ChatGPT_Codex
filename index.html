THE THINKING BEHIND THE PLAYBOOK

Make every touch earn its place.

This is a proposed approach for an interview, not a claim about past results. All companies, signals and metrics are fictional.

What I would move away from

Generic feature-led emails, email-only campaigns, giving up after two touches, and follow-ups that add no new value.

What I would test

Verified buying signals, outcome-led messages, varied calling times, multiple stakeholders and relevant multichannel follow-up.

Where AI helps

Summarise research, suggest account tiers, draft role-specific messages and compare campaign results. This demo uses curated examples; it does not call an AI API.

Where I own the decision

Verify sources, validate hypotheses, review every message, respect consent and opt-outs, and decide when an account deserves more effort.

Weekly rhythm: refresh signals on Monday, protect daily calling blocks, review objections with peers, and optimise for qualified pipeline on Friday.
